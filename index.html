BrightConnect Voice Assistant

Click the "Call Assistant" button to speak with our AI-powered voice assistant. Here's what it can help you with:

1

"My internet is not working"

  • Asks for your ZIP code to check service status
  • Checks our outage database in real-time
  • If outage found: Reports estimated resolution time and reason
  • If no outage: Offers to create a support ticket
  • Can sign you up for SMS notifications when service is restored
2

"I want to pay my bill"

  • Asks for your name and account phone number
  • Verifies your identity by confirming the name on the account
  • Retrieves your current balance and due date
  • Offers to send a secure payment link via SMS or Email
  • Provides a confirmation code for your records
3

"Check my ticket status"

  • Asks for your phone number to look up tickets
  • Finds your most recent support tickets
  • Reports ticket ID, status, and issue type
  • Provides updates on open or pending tickets
4

Other Requests (Escalation)

  • Cancellation requests
  • Plan changes or upgrades
  • Billing disputes
  • Technical issues requiring human support

These requests are automatically escalated by creating a support ticket with high priority for our team to follow up.
